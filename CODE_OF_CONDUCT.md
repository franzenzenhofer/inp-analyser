# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others'
private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a GitHub issue, or privately via the maintainer's
GitHub profile (https://github.com/franzenzenhofer). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially where possible. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow
this Code of Conduct.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
